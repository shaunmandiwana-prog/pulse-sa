Institutional FAQ

Enterprise Compliance & Architecture

Answers to common questions from Credit Risk Committees, Internal Audit, and IT Architecture teams.

How does Pulse SA ensure full compliance with the POPIA Act (Act 4 of 2013)?
All data is collected following an explicit informed consent protocol. Agents verbally explain the mapping purpose before recording details. Personal identification documents are inspected visually for existence and validity; 13-digit ID numbers are never stored in plain text or sold.
How does the data integrate with our Loan Origination System (LOS)?
We provide standard REST API endpoints (`/v1/traders/:id/credit-profile`), automated Webhook notifications when a trader achieves "Credit-Ready" status, and secure SFTP batch CSV delivery for core banking batch runs.
How does the DQS (Data Quality Score) prevent ghost traders and falsified surveys?
Our DQS engine runs automated checks across 3 independent factors: hardware GPS coordinate clustering, physical receipt image matching, and repeat foot-traffic observations by different agents over time. Anomaly detection flags statistical outliers before credit limits are generated.
Can we commission bespoke surveys in specific municipal wards?
Yes. Using our Municipal Demarcation Board (MDB 2026) ward layer, institutional clients can deploy targeted micro-gigs to any of South Africa's 4,488 wards with active agent coverage deployed in under 72 hours.
