Pulse SA
Intelligence Layer · Est. 2025

South Africa's first structured intelligence network for the informal economy.

Our Mission

To make South Africa's informal economy visible, bankable, and insurable — one data point at a time.

Our Vision

A South Africa where every street vendor, spaza shop, and township trader has access to the financial services that formal businesses take for granted.

Why Your Work Matters

The data you collect today helps banks offer loans to traders who've never had one, helps insurers price cover for spaza shops, and helps companies serve communities that have been invisible for too long.

You are not just earning points. You are building the infrastructure of economic inclusion.
