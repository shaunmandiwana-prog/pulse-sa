Agent Terms & POPIA Agreement

1. Accuracy & Integrity Policy All survey data, price readings, and receipt photos submitted must be 100% genuine and observed in person. Submitting fake, altered, or duplicate data results in immediate account forfeiture and point confiscation.
2. POPIA & Trader Consent Agents must verbally explain the purpose of the data collection to informal traders and respect their privacy. Personal ID numbers are verified visually and never recorded directly without consent.
3. Rewards & Payouts Pulse Points are earned upon automated verification (DQS audit) and are redeemable for airtime, retail vouchers, or bank cashout per published redemption tiers.
4. Independent Contractor Status Pulse Agents operate as independent field researchers and gig participants, earning rewards per completed verified task.

Our Mission

To make South Africa's informal economy visible, bankable, and insurable - one data point at a time.

Our Vision

A South Africa where every street vendor, spaza shop, and township trader has access to the financial services that formal businesses take for granted.

Why Your Work Matters

The data you collect today helps banks offer loans to traders who've never had one, helps insurers price cover for spaza shops, and helps companies serve communities that have been invisible for too long.

You are not just earning points. You are building the infrastructure of economic inclusion.
