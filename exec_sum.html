3. Forensic Cash Audit Methodology (ISA 500 & ISA 240)

Under International Standards on Auditing, uncorroborated cash sales represent high inherent risk. Pulse SA overcomes this through an empirical 3-Factor Triangulation Model:

  • Upstream Wholesale Invoices (COGS): AI Vision Neural OCR scans formal printed tax invoices from Metro, Boxer, and Abattoirs, extracting VAT numbers, dates, and itemized SKUs. Reconstructed Revenue = Audited Wholesale Purchases × (1 + Standard Township Markup %).
  • Longitudinal Foot-Traffic Audit: Independent spot-checks by multiple agents across morning, midday, and evening trading windows confirm active customer volume. Cash Velocity = Customers/Hr × Trading Hours × Avg Kasi Basket Size (R35).
  • Route Distributor Drop-Sheets: Photo verification of daily bread (Sasko/Albany) and beverage (Coca-Cola) bakkie delivery carbon notes proving inventory turnover without bank statements.
